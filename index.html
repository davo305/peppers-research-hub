📋 Community Rules

Help us keep Pepper Collective organized, useful and respectful.

🤝 1. Be Respectful

Treat members with respect. No harassment, bullying, hate speech or personal attacks.

🔬 2. Research & Education

Keep research discussions educational and informative. Clearly separate research information from personal opinions.

📝 3. No Conversations Inside Topics

Please do not have conversations inside topic threads. Topics are designed to organize information so members can quickly find what they are looking for without scrolling through long conversations.

🚫 4. No Selling or Reselling Discussions

Do not use the community to discuss selling or reselling. Keep general community discussions focused on research, education and information sharing.

📣 5. Promotion Requires Admin Approval

Anyone wishing to promote a vendor, product, service, group, website or other commercial offering must first contact an admin and receive approval.

💬 6. Keep It Helpful

Ask questions, share useful information and help keep the community welcoming and organized.

🛡️ 7. Follow Admin & Telegram Rules

Follow moderator instructions and Telegram's applicable rules and policies.
