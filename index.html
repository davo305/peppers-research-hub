📋 Community Rules

🤝 1. Be Respectful

Treat members with respect. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats or personal attacks.

🔬 2. Research & Education

Keep research discussions focused on education, information sharing and community learning.

📝 3. No Conversations Inside Topics

Please do not have conversations inside topic threads. Topics are designed to organize information so members can quickly find what they are looking for. Use general chat for conversations and follow-up discussion.

🚫 4. No Unauthorized Selling or Reselling

Selling, reselling, promotional activity, advertising or offers must not be posted without admin approval.

📣 5. Promotion Requires Admin Approval

Anyone wishing to sell, promote, advertise or offer products or services must contact an admin and receive approval first.

⚠️ 6. Third-Party Responsibility

Pepper Collective and Peppers Research Hub do not guarantee independent vendors, group buys, services, products or transactions. Members are responsible for conducting their own research and due diligence.

📚 7. Keep Information Organized

Use the appropriate areas so research and community information remains easy to find.
