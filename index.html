📋 Community Rules

🤝 1. Be Respectful

Treat members with respect. No harassment, bullying, hate speech or personal attacks.

🔬 2. Research & Education

Keep research discussions educational and informative. Clearly separate research information from personal opinions.

📝 3. No Conversations Inside Topics

Please do not have conversations inside topic threads. Topics are designed to organize information so members can quickly find what they are looking for.

🚫 4. No Unauthorized Selling or Reselling

Selling, reselling, promotional activity, advertising, or offers must not be posted without admin approval.

📣 5. Promotion Requires Admin Approval

Anyone wishing to sell, promote, advertise, or offer products or services must contact an admin and receive approval first.

⚠️ 6. Third-Party Responsibility

Pepper Collective and Peppers Research Hub do not guarantee independent vendors, services, or transactions. Members are responsible for conducting their own due diligence.

📚 7. Keep Information Organized

Use the appropriate areas so research and community information remains easy to find.
