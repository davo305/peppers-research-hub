🌶️ Community Rules

1. 🤝 Be Respectful

Treat all members with respect. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks.

2. 🔬 Research & Education

Keep research discussions focused on education, information sharing, and community learning. Clearly separate published information from personal opinions or experiences.

3. 📝 Do Not Have Conversations Inside Topics

Topic threads are designed to organize information so members can quickly find what they are looking for. Please do not fill topic threads with ongoing conversations. Use the appropriate general chat area for conversations and follow-up discussions.

4. 🚫 No Unauthorized Selling or Reselling Discussions

Do not use the community or research topics for unauthorized selling, reselling, pricing discussions, advertisements, or transactions.

5. ✅ Admin Approval Required for Promotions

Anyone who wants to sell, promote, advertise, or offer products or services within the community must contact an admin and receive approval first. Unauthorized promotional posts may be removed.

6. ⚠️ Third-Party Responsibility

Pepper Collective and Peppers Research Hub do not guarantee independent third-party vendors, services, transactions, or arrangements. Members are responsible for conducting their own due diligence.

7. 📚 Keep Information Organized

Post information in the appropriate area and help keep the community clean, organized, and easy to search.

8. 🌶️ Help Protect the Community

Avoid spam, scams, impersonation, misleading links, and behavior that could put members or the community at unnecessary risk.
