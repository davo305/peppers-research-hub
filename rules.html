📋 Community Guidelines

Pepper Collective Community Rules

🌶️ Welcome to Pepper Collective

We're here to learn, share research, exchange information and build a respectful community. Please review the guidelines below before participating.

🤝 1. Be Respectful

Treat all members with respect. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats or personal attacks.

🧪 2. Research & Education

Keep research-related discussions focused on education, information sharing and community knowledge. Clearly separate established information from personal opinions or experiences.

📝 3. Use Topics Correctly

Please use community topics for their intended purpose so information remains organized and easy for members to locate. Use the appropriate discussion areas for general conversations and follow-up questions.

💬 4. Keep It Helpful

Ask questions, share useful information and contribute constructively. Avoid excessive spam, repeated posts or content that disrupts the community.

🏪 5. Vendors & Third Parties

Third-party vendors, resources and group buys may be discussed or shared within designated areas. Pepper Collective and Peppers Research Hub do not independently verify or guarantee third-party vendors, products, transactions, payments, shipping, quality or services.

🔒 6. Respect Member Privacy

Do not publicly share another member's private information, messages, contact information or other personal details without their permission.

🚫 7. No Scams or Misleading Information

Scamming, impersonation, intentionally misleading members or fraudulent activity is not permitted.

🛡️ 8. Moderator Decisions

Moderators may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community, maintain organization or enforce these guidelines.
