🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Peppers Research Hub respects the privacy of its visitors. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, why it may be collected and how that information may be used when you visit or interact with this website.

1. Information You Provide

Peppers Research Hub may collect information that you voluntarily submit through forms on the website.

Our Peppers Backup Contact List currently allows visitors to provide:

  • Email address
  • Telegram username, if voluntarily provided
  • Consent to receive community-related communications

You are not required to provide a Telegram username to browse the website.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the backup contact form may be used to:

  • Send important Peppers Research Hub announcements
  • Provide replacement Pepper Collective community links if an existing community becomes unavailable
  • Communicate important changes involving the website or community
  • Respond to communications or administrative inquiries

We do not intend to sell personal information submitted through the backup contact list.

3. Form Processing

The Peppers Backup Contact List uses a third-party form processing service to receive form submissions.

Information submitted through the form may therefore be transmitted to and processed by that service according to its own privacy practices.

Visitors should avoid submitting sensitive personal, financial or medical information through the backup contact form.

4. Third-Party Websites

Peppers Research Hub contains links to websites and services operated by independent third parties.

These may include research resources, suppliers, educational websites, videos, vendors, group buys, social platforms and other external resources.

Once you leave Peppers Research Hub, the privacy practices of the external website or service apply.

Peppers Research Hub does not control the privacy practices, security or data collection practices of independent third-party websites.

5. Affiliate Links

Peppers Research Hub may participate in affiliate programs.

Some links on the website may therefore be affiliate links. If a visitor follows an affiliate link and makes a qualifying purchase, Peppers Research Hub may receive a commission without increasing the visitor's purchase price.

Affiliate relationships will be disclosed where appropriate.

6. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Peppers Research Hub itself may not require visitors to create an account in order to browse its public content.

Third-party services linked to or integrated with the website may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

If additional analytics, advertising or tracking technologies are added to Peppers Research Hub in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information submitted through the website.

However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Peppers Research Hub is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Individuals under the age required to independently consent to data collection in their jurisdiction should not submit personal information through the website.

9. Your Choices

If you previously joined the Peppers Backup Contact List and would like to request removal of your submitted contact information, contact us using the email address below.

Please provide enough information for us to reasonably identify the contact information you want removed.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website, its features or applicable practices change.

The date shown at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last updated.
